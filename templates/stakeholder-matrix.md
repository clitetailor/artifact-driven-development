## Power-Interest Map

```
         High Influence
              |
   Monitor    |   Manage Closely
              |
 ─────────────┼───────────────────
              |
   Keep       |   Keep Satisfied
  Informed    |
              |
         Low Influence
  Low Interest ──────── High Interest
```

- **Manage Closely** — High influence, high interest. Full engagement.
- **Keep Satisfied** — High influence, low interest. Minimal effort, high impact.
- **Keep Informed** — Low influence, high interest. Regular updates.
- **Monitor** — Low influence, low interest. Passive awareness.
